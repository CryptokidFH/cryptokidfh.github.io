pyrava

A zero-dependency Python client for Barava smart lamps, written from the vendor's protocol notes and then corrected against firmware 1.0.1 on real hardware. It covers discovery, the deferred-reply keepalive cycle, the five LED zones, and an animation compiler, with a command line for driving all of it.
